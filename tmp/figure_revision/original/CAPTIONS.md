# Figure captions (PHUSE EU 2026)

All figures are generated by `scripts/paper_figures.py` from locked or released artifacts only. To regenerate them, run `.venv-figures/Scripts/python.exe scripts/paper_figures.py`. Each figure is written as vector PDF, as SVG, and as 600-dpi PNG.

**Figure 1. From protocol PDF to a blind-scored feasibility forecast.**
- Historical registry results are converted into a statistical evidence table (106,185 observation rows) and into fitted probability parameter tables (4,693 V3 parameters).
- Separate operational and safety assets are fitted on their own registry corpora.
- The protocol enters only at the StudySpec stage. It supplies rules, not distributions.
- Every stage from StudySpec to report is locked with SHA-256 checksums of its outputs, inputs and code before any registry result is fetched.

**Figure 2. One registry observation, three probability summaries.**
- (a) Provenance chain for NCT00091572: registry group EG000 (124 serious-AE patients of 419 at risk) → evidence row `d9b934b17937e424` → V3 parameter `3b1473de78a0a559`. The safety denominator (419) is kept separate from the efficacy denominator.
- (b) Three distributions:
  - the within-study Beta posterior, from this study only;
  - the hierarchical population posterior;
  - the probability for a new study.

  (b) and (c) are drawn from the 400 stored posterior draws, not reconstructed from quantiles. The future-study interval is deliberately wide; it is a limitation, not an error.
- (c) Share of each node's posterior precision borrowed from its parent level. This is a model diagnostic, not a share of patients.

**Figure 3. Protocol-conditioned simulation, development protocol ACNS0332 (unblinded).**
- (a) Eligibility criteria by how they can be evaluated. Most criteria use variables the population does not generate. Patients are therefore UNDETERMINED rather than assumed eligible: 0 of 10,000 are proven eligible.
- (b) Enrolment under the two accrual rates stated in the protocol.
- (c) The locked control-arm event-free survival curve, the prediction at 5 years with its 90% interval, and the registry value with its 95% CI.
- This protocol was used during development, and its registry results were read before the final run.

**Figure 4. Order of events in the blind test.**
- For each sealed trial, prediction-stage lock times are shown relative to that trial's registry fetch (time 0).
- Every prediction lock precedes the fetch. The script asserts this, and the unsealing step verifies it.
- Comparison stages are locked after the fetch, by design. BLIND_1's earliest lock is its protocol-facts stage, reused by the final chain.

**Figure 5. Recruitment: the protocol's assumption, the pipeline forecast and the outcome (blind).**
- (a) The pipeline's locked accrual-rate forecast (50% and 80% intervals, with the log-normal reconstruction shaded), the rate the protocol assumed or stated, and the observed rate.
  - The observed rate is enrolled patients divided by the registry's start-to-primary-completion window, so it is a lower bound.
  - BLIND_3 enrolled one patient and is not scoreable.
- (b) Probability the failure model gave to the outcome that occurred, against the base rate. Neither terminated trial was flagged above base rate.

**Figure 6. Registry-scale calibration on held-out trials.**
- (a) Coverage at each nominal interval level. Marker size scales with the number of held-out observations. The shaded band is the pre-set pass band.
  - Points above the band are intervals wider than needed.
  - The survival backtest includes the evaluated trial in its HR posterior, so it is a consistency check only.
- (b) Mean log score for held-out adverse-event counts: V2 (in-sample), V3.1 before calibration, V3.1 after calibration. V3.1 used a 60/20/20 fit/calibrate/report split.

**Figure 7. Individual adverse events in the blind trials.**
- Predicted 90% intervals are evaluated at the registry's number at risk, shown against the observed proportions.
- 10 of 10 (BLIND_1, n = 7) and 31 of 33 (BLIND_2, n = 54) matched terms fall inside their intervals. The intervals are wide.

**Figure 8. The informative estimates missed.**
- (a, b) Estimates of the proportion with any serious adverse event:
  - the pooled headline for the protocol's own subgroup;
  - the breakdown by age group, phase and drug-class overlap, with the number of evidence studies;
  - the observed proportion with its Wilson 95% CI.

  The headline missed in both trials: 58.8% vs 2/7 and 14.5% vs 26/54.
- (c) BLIND_1 control-arm median PFS: the locked evidence-derived prediction (4.5 months, 3 studies) against the registry value (2.1 months, n = 6, upper CI not reached).
