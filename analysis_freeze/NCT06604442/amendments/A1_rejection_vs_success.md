# Amendment A1: two-sided rejection vs positive-direction success

Computed after the reveal from frozen inputs only (the design SD; the frozen master tables). The protocol's test is a two-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test at alpha 0.05. 'Rejection' = p < 0.05 (either direction); 'success' = p < 0.05 with the comparator (piflufolastat) higher. At effect 0 the rejection rate is the type I error; the success rate is about half of it and must not be reported as a type I error.
